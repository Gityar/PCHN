Education and service for the health of every child

The Pediatrics and Child Health Nursing Education and Service unit of the College of Medicine and Health Sciences, Wollo University, operates from the Tertiary Care & Teaching Hospital Campus. The unit prepares competent pediatric nurses, supports evidence-based practice at the bedside, and coordinates child-health services for the families we serve.

Staff members register once to receive a verified profile, and administrators issue the single-use registration codes that make that verification possible.
